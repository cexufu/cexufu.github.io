# AI & Platform Governance — Cexu (Tony) Fu

I build **end-to-end governance systems** for AI platforms: risk taxonomy & standards → policy & workflows → evaluation & auditability.

## Pillars
1. **Standards & Risk Taxonomy** — multi-layer standards (human/account/content/agents) with graded harms & exemptions.
2. **Governance Engineering** — reviewer/ML division of labor, explainability, rollout/rollback, incident runbooks.
3. **Evaluation & Benchmarks** — harmfulness, consistency/reasoning, diversity/evenness, contradiction density (HITL).
4. **Integrity & Polarization** — info cocoons, cross-stance responsiveness, coordinated behaviors.
5. **Compliance & Transparency** — DPIA, decision traceability, transparency reporting.
