AI governance, grounded in mechanisms.

I'm Cexu (Tony) Fu. I answer governance questions with evidence from inside the model: tracing how information flows through it, attributing outputs to the inputs that drive them, and testing those attributions by intervention.

PhD researcher in the EPSRC Centre for Doctoral Training in Cyber Security, University of Bristol. Before that, content safety and platform governance at ByteDance/Douyin, Tencent and Kuaishou.

Questions I work on

{% include fig-attribution.html %}

Where did this output come from?

Attributing generated text to the source passages and internal computations that produced it, and checking each attribution with ablations rather than taking a score at face value.

{% include fig-leak.html %}

Did confidential input leak?

Following sensitive content as it moves through a model or an agent, so leakage can be detected from the mechanism and not only from the final text.

{% include fig-verify.html %}

Can this claim about a model be verified?

Turning governance requirements, such as labelling, review and data-protection rules, into tests with an explicit standard of evidence.
